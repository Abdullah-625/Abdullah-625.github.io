Things to Avoid or Limit

These factors may make focus and daily routines harder for some people.

1
🛋️

Inactivity

Regular movement and exercise may support attention, mood, and overall well-being.

2
📱

Excessive Screen Time

Endless scrolling and notifications can interrupt focus. Try setting boundaries that work for you.

3
🍩

Lots of Sugary, Refined Foods

A balanced eating pattern can support steady energy. Food affects everyone differently.

4
🗃️

Cluttered Environments

A simple, organized workspace can reduce visual distractions and make tasks easier to start.

5
😴

Lack of Sleep

Too little sleep can make attention, mood, and daily functioning more difficult.

✓

Build These Habits

You don't have to change everything at once. Pick one small thing and begin there.

1
🗓️

Keep a Consistent Routine

A familiar daily rhythm can make the day feel more predictable.

2
📝

Break Tasks into Steps

Turn a big task into small actions you can begin right now.

3
📆

Use a Planner or Calendar

Write down appointments, deadlines, and reminders instead of holding them in your head.

4
🪴

Organize Your Space

Give everyday items a home and clear just one small area at a time.

5
👟

Move Your Body

Walking, dancing, sports, or stretching all count. Choose movement you enjoy.

6
🥗

Eat a Balanced Diet

Include a variety of foods, such as vegetables, fruit, protein, and whole grains.

7
☕

Be Mindful of Sugar & Caffeine

Notice how they affect your energy and sleep; avoid relying on caffeine to replace rest.

8
🛏️

Keep a Sleep Schedule

A regular bedtime and wake-up time can help support good sleep.

9
🌙

Create a Calming Bedtime Routine

Try reading, quiet music, or another relaxing activity before bed.

10
🧘

Try Mindfulness

Gentle breathing or meditation may help some people manage stress and refocus.

11
🌿

Manage Stress

Make space for hobbies, breaks, supportive conversations, and professional help when needed.

12
🫂

Seek Support

Trusted friends, family, support groups, or a qualified professional can help.

13
🎯

Set Realistic Goals

Choose a small, clear next step and count progress rather than perfection.

14
⏱️

Use Timers & Alarms

Try a short timer to get started or reminders to switch tasks and take breaks.

💙
A gentle reminder
This page shares general wellness ideas, not medical advice. ADHD affects people differently, and lifestyle habits are not a substitute for professional assessment or treatment. You don't need to do everything perfectly—start with what feels manageable.
